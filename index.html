Mystery Fossil Tooth: Argumentation Evidence Card Sort

Work with your group to evaluate how evidence relates to each scientific claim.

Group Members
Scientific Question
From what kind of animal did this fossil tooth come?
Directions: For each activity, drag the evidence cards into Supports the claim, Might support the claim, or Does not support the claim. Discuss why you placed each card there. The goal is to reason about the evidence—not simply finish the sort. On a touch device, tap a card and then tap a category.
Language of Argumentation
This evidence supports the claim because…This evidence might support the claim because…This evidence does not support the claim because…I agree because…I disagree because…Do you agree? Why or why not?
Final Discussion
After completing both sorts: Which claim do you think is better supported by the available evidence? Explain which evidence is strongest and why.
